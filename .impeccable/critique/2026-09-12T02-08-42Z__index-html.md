## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment (Assessment A):** Wards is authored for a real, narrow workflow, not skinned from a generic notes template. The A.P. split (Comorbidades/M.U.C/Cirurgias), Planos separated into residente/preceptor, Evoluções deliberately built as one continuous scrollable text instead of discrete entries, and silent lab-value extraction from free evolution text into structured Exames records are all decisions nobody makes for a generic app — they encode how a resident actually rounds and charts. The handwriting tool's zoom-box magnifier (writes large, commits ink at real scale, auto-advances) is a non-trivial feature built specifically for Apple Pencil charting at bedside. It slips into generic territory at the seams: native unstyled `prompt()`/`confirm()`/`alert()` handle discharge, deletion, and most feedback — a jarring departure from the composed Almond Cream / Dark Slate Grey system everywhere else.

**Deterministic scan (Assessment B):** `impeccable detect --json` reports the project **clean** under its default config (exit 0, zero findings). Re-run with `--no-config`, the detector surfaces exactly one rule — `overused-font` (Inter, `css/app.css:51`) — which the project's own `.impeccable/config.json` already waives with a documented, deliberate rationale (Inter was a considered substitution, recorded from an earlier session). No false positives to flag beyond that already-adjudicated one. In short: the automated pass found nothing your own project config hasn't already reasoned about — every issue below came from human review, not the linter.

**Visual overlays:** Attempted on 6 representative views (patient list, HDA, Sinais Vitais, Evoluções, the handwriting tool, and the monthly report) via the bundled live-server + `detect.js` injection. All 6 failed for the same reason: the deployed app is HTTPS and the local injection server is HTTP-only, so the browser's mixed-content policy blocked every injection (`net::ERR_FAILED`, confirmed also via a direct `fetch()` test). This is a transport limitation of critiquing a live HTTPS deployment against this tool, not a signal about the app itself — no user-visible overlay is available for this run; treat the deterministic CLI scan above as the full extent of automated evidence.

## Overall Impression

The information architecture is the strongest thing here — it's shaped by an actual rounding workflow, not a generic CRUD template, and the handwriting/zoom-box tool is a genuine, cared-for centerpiece. The gap is entirely in *feedback and finish*: nothing confirms that a save, a sync, or a silent background action (lab extraction, diagnosis fuzzy-matching) actually happened, and at the two moments that most need composure — discharging and deleting a patient — the app drops out of its own visual language into raw OS dialogs. The single biggest opportunity is closing that feedback loop: a resident using this mid-shift, interrupted constantly, needs to *see* that something saved before moving to the next bed.

## What's Working

- **Lab-value auto-extraction from free text** (`extrairLabsDoTexto`/`mesclarLabsHoje` in `js/app.js`): typing "K 4,0 Na 138" into an ordinary evolution note silently creates/merges a structured Exames record with recognizable pill chips — removes duplicate entry without asking the user to change how they naturally write.
- **Zoom-box magnifier in the handwriting tool** (`js/handwriting.js`): a physically-motivated, non-generic interaction — write large in a lens, ink commits small on the real page, the window auto-advances before you hit the edge rather than after. Real design effort went into this.
- **Domain-shaped IA**: Planos split residente/preceptor, A.P. split into Comorbidades/M.U.C./Cirurgias — mirrors actual chart structure rather than a generic "notes" bucket.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Global FAB ignores tab context and creates a demonstrated mis-tap hazard**
- **Why it matters**: The `+ Exame` FAB renders unconditionally on all 8 tabs, including Planos, Prescrições, Pareceres, A.P, and Sinais Vitais, where it's irrelevant to the tab's task. Concretely reproduced live: saving a handwriting annotation on HDA re-renders the view with the FAB sitting exactly where "Salvar" was just tapped — a natural second tap (to confirm the save landed) hits "+ Exame" instead and silently navigates away.
- **Fix**: Make the FAB context-aware (hide/repurpose per tab — it's redundant with the vitals "Adicionar" button on Sinais Vitais already), or reposition it off the just-used action's coordinates.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Native browser dialogs break the design system at the highest-stakes moments**
- **Why it matters**: Discharge (`prompt()`), delete (`confirm()`), and most feedback (`alert()`) all fall back to unstyled OS chrome — verified live. This is the one place the calm, composed Almond Cream system disappears, and it happens exactly when closing out a patient or permanently deleting a record.
- **Fix**: Build a lightweight in-system modal/toast matching the pill/card language for confirm, prompt-for-text, and toast feedback.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Secondary-action buttons are visually indistinguishable from disabled controls**
- **Why it matters**: `.btn-secondary` (`--accent-soft` on `--canvas`) styles "Salvar" (HDA/Prescrições), "Escrever à mão," and "Adicionar" (A.P., Planos, Pareceres) — all frequent, real actions — yet reads as muted/greyed-out. The identical "Adicionar" action is `btn-primary` on Sinais Vitais: an unintentional hierarchy inversion plus a cross-tab inconsistency.
- **Fix**: Raise `--accent-soft` contrast, or restyle as a proper outlined secondary button; standardize which action-type gets primary vs. secondary app-wide.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

**[P2] Muted text sits below WCAG AA contrast**
- **Why it matters**: `--ink-mute` (#736d57) on canvas/surface computes to ≈4.0:1, and the warn pill combo to ≈3.97:1 — both under the 4.5:1 AA minimum for normal text, and both carry real clinical content (patient card subtitles, section titles, timestamps, status pills).
- **Fix**: Darken `--ink-mute` and `--warn` a few percentage points; re-verify against both canvas and surface.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P3] Diagnosis category matching (0.9 similarity threshold) risks silently fragmenting the monthly report**
- **Why it matters**: `Matching.resolverCategoria` only merges a new discharge diagnosis into an existing category above a strict 0.9 trigram similarity — "AVC isquêmico," "AVCi," and "AVC isquêmico agudo" will likely land as three separate categories in the exact report this feature exists to produce, with no screen to merge/rename categories after the fact.
- **Fix**: Lower the threshold, or add a small category-management view reachable from the report.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user — the resident doing this every day on rounds)**: The FAB mis-tap (P1) costs a real detour multiple times a shift, precisely because Alex taps confidently at remembered coordinates rather than re-reading labels. No way to duplicate/carry forward yesterday's Planos or vitals — every patient, every day, is manual re-entry, despite the app's own archive logic already tracking a 7-day cadence internally. The 8-tab horizontal scroll (Prescrições/Pareceres sit off-screen by default) adds friction to Alex's highest-frequency action: jumping straight to one tab per patient.

**Riley (stress-tester — rushed, interrupted mid-task)**: Saving an evolution gives no confirmation — if Riley is paged away mid-save, there's no visual proof either way. The discharge `prompt()` is a blocking native modal; a fat-fingered dismiss under pressure silently abandons the discharge with the admission just staying "Ativo," no explanation. Delete is fast (one swipe + one confirm), which combined with the FAB mis-tap raises the odds of a nervous double-tap landing somewhere destructive-adjacent.

## Minor Observations

- `viewReport`'s month header renders "Setembro De 2026" — `text-transform:capitalize` capitalizes the lowercase "de" from `toLocaleDateString`, a visible copy bug in production.
- M.U.C. placeholder truncates at default width: "Adicionar medicação de uso" (cuts off "contínuo").
- Patient list card shows iniciais and motivoAdmissao in identical `.sub` styling with no visual distinction between "who" and "why."
- Auto-created lab exam records default to "Sem resumo" — correct, but a pills-only record with no summary looks unfinished on the Exames list.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the FAB became the *save* action for whatever tab is active, so its position is always the highest-value action rather than a fixed trap?
- What if the topbar kept a lightweight "last saved: 2 min ago" indicator, given how sync-anxious offline-first clinical documentation naturally is?
- What if discharge's HD-final prompt became an in-system sheet reusing the fuzzy-match already computed by `Matching` ("did you mean: AVC isquêmico, used 6x?") — turning the fragmentation risk into a feature?
- What if the tab bar collapsed to a swipeable full-width paging control instead of a horizontally-scrolling pill row, given 8 tabs already exceeds an iPhone width?
